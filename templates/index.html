DATA INSIGHTS

What Makes a Video Go Viral?

Based on analysis of 12,000 videos in our dataset (20% labeled viral)

📊

Viral Threshold

Videos labeled viral start from ~735,000 views. Every video above 1 million views in the dataset is viral. Non-viral videos never exceed this range.

👥

Biggest Factor: Subscribers

Channel size is the strongest predictor. Channels with 1M+ subscribers have a 61% viral rate. Below 100k subscribers the rate drops close to zero.

🔥

Category Impact

Music (57%) and Comedy (35%) dominate. Sports and Gaming also perform well. Vlogs, Travel, and Education have the lowest viral rates.

⏱️

Shorter Performs Better

Videos under 5 minutes have a 38% viral rate. Longer videos (10+ min) drop to around 15%. Duration has a clear negative correlation with going viral.

👍

Engagement Volume Matters

Absolute likes, comments, and shares are strongly linked to viral status. Interestingly, engagement rates (likes per view) are almost the same for viral and non-viral videos.

🖼️

Thumbnail Face Helps Slightly

Videos with a face visible in the thumbnail have a modestly higher viral rate (23% vs 18%). Upload day and hour show almost no meaningful difference.

How the model works: A Logistic Regression model trained on duration, title length, thumbnail face, upload hour/day, tags, description length, subscriber count, views, likes, comments, shares, and video category. It predicts the probability that a video will cross the viral threshold based on patterns learned from the dataset.
